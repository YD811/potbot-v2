**Tweet 4 — POT DUELS** *(most viral tweet — add image of the slide)*

> But the real alpha: ⚔️ POT Duels.
> 
> Any POT can challenge another.
> Both vaults stake a % into on-chain escrow.
> Spectators bet SOL on who wins.
> After 48h — better returns win. Everything pays out on-chain. Automatically.
> 
> Group DeFi as sport.
> Zero custodians. Zero trust needed.

**Tweet 5 — SIDE BETS**

> The side bet mechanic is what makes this viral.
> 
> You don't need to be IN a POT to participate in a Duel.
> Any Telegram user can bet SOL on the outcome.
> 
> Your friend group debates the duel in the group chat.
> People pile in bets.
> Winners share the result.
> New POTs get created.
> 
> That's the flywheel.
