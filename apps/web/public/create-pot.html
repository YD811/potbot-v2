🛡️ Privacy Architecture
🔐
Shielded Balances (Umbra SDK)
Vault balance and member holdings are encrypted on-chain. Only members with viewing keys can see the financials.
🕵️
Private Swaps (PrivacyCash ZK)
Trade via ZK-routed Jupiter swaps. On-chain observers see a proof but never the asset, amount, or counterparty.
🧩
MagicBlock Private Ephemeral Rollup
Governance proposals and votes execute inside a Private Ephemeral Rollup. Strategies, positions, and votes stay off public state until settled.
🔑
Invite-Only Access
New members join exclusively via your unique invite link. No public listing. Treat it as a closed alpha club for your trusted circle.
📋
Viewing Keys for Compliance
Generate selective disclosure keys for accountants or regulators. Decrypt exactly what's needed — nothing more. Full audit trail on demand.
🏛️
Private Governance
Proposals and vote tallies are only visible to pot members. Outsiders see no signal about strategy direction or internal activity.
